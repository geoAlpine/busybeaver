## 2. The doubling mechanism — the rightward comb-repack sweep `[PROVEN]`

The rightward sweep is the 2-transition cycle `E:0→1RF`, `F:1→1RE`: over a comb `(01)^k` the
head reads `E` on every `0`, `F` on every `1`, rewriting `(01)^k → 1^{2k}` and advancing +2 per
`E`. By 2-transition induction this holds for every k (the standard sweep certificate). This is
the ×2 (doubling) engine: combs repack into blocks. The sweep is **phase-locked** to the `(01)`
lattice; it breaks phase only at a *gap* (two adjacent 0s), which is exactly a halt-gate
exposure. `x2c_milestone.py` reads the reachable "rest" grammar (head C at the right frontier):
a cascade `[low comb] 1^{2^k−2} 0 [middle] 0 1^2 0` with **all rest gaps ∈ {1,2}**.

## 4. Why the closure does NOT terminate to a halt-free set — the OBSTRUCTION `[PROVEN]`

**(a) The halt pattern is dense on the tape, not absent.** `x2c_census.py` (full-tape gap census,
every step to 2·10⁵, and sparse to 3·10⁷): gaps of **every** length occur on the tape, including
**odd** `3,5,7,9,…`. A gap of **exactly 3** is present *somewhere* on the tape at **4 896 / 200 000
≈ 2.4 %** of steps (first mature-regime occurrence at step 1 136 922). So the safety property is
**NOT** the static/regular statement "no gap-3 exists on the tape" — gap-3 exists constantly. It
is the **dynamic** statement "at the instant the *rightward E-scanner* arrives at a gap, that gap
is not 3" — a coupling between the head's phase and the odometer's local carry structure.

**(b) No global parity is conserved.** `x2c_phase.py` (50 000 E-gap events): of 7 candidate
conserved parities (head position, #1s left/right/total, frontier offsets), **every one splits
≈50/50** across the even-gap events — while all confirm 0 odd gaps. The even-gap invariant is
therefore *not* a global mod-2 invariant; it is structural/phase-local to the block lattice.

The closure is finite at every radius, yet the **HALT window is reachable at every R ≤ 8**
