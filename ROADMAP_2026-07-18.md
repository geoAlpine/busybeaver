## 2. BB(6) — where the wall is (a description, not a work plan)

`Completion.lean` proves `BB6_eq_championSteps` conditionally on **11 explicit axioms** ✓
(`o4_ledger`, `seed`, `NormalityPQ`, `o7orbit`, `snOrbit`, `o17_nonhalt`,
`holdouts1087_nonhalt`, `championSteps`, `BB6`, `champion_lower`, `enumeration_upper`). This is
a bookkeeping assembly (`BB6 = championSteps` by antisymmetry of `≤`), not a proof of any
halting fact; only o4 is Lean end-to-end (`o4_reduction`).

BB(6) is blocked, at **mathematics scale**, by several mutually irreducible open problems:

| block | machines | class |
|---|---|---|
| (i) single-orbit base-3/2, 4/3, 8/3 normality = **a strict sub-fragment of the Andrieu–Eliahou–Vivion / Mahler 3/2 conjecture** | 14 | the (K) wall — famous open |
| (ii) generalized-Collatz **2^k-avoidance** (o7, Space Needle) | 2 | different open problem; not implied by normality |
| (iii) **o17 gate-timing** (unbounded gate-state) | 1 | its own irreducible form |
| (iv) ~1087-holdout enumeration + `champion_lower` / `enumeration_upper` | — | **engineering-scale**, but not certified cryptid-free |

**The (K) wall, stated precisely:** a *specified-single-orbit, one-sided, low-level* fragment
of the AEV normality conjecture / Mahler's 3/2 problem — strictly weaker than the published
conjecture, but beyond every unconditional result (nothing gives one-sided `liminf > 0` for any
specific such orbit). Do not state it as "equivalent to AEV Conj 1.6" nor as "implied by known
results."

**The impossibility meta-theorems** (no-structure-only-selection, adelic sub-action closure,
AIU neutral-direction, EUE coisometry no-go, excursion/Kac supermartingale, L_ann
odd-annihilation) are **rigorous no-gos for named technique families**, each explicitly
disclaiming any statement about (K)'s truth or decidability. They prove the obstruction is
orbit-specific for the structural registers; they do **not** prove BB(6) unprovable. State them
as "proven impossible for method-class X," never as "proven impossible." The global "no weapon
works" claim is a heuristic organizing principle over ~20 routes, not a theorem.

Granting (i)–(iii) as axioms would leave only (iv), which is engineering-scale — but the 1087
set is not yet certified free of new hard cases.
