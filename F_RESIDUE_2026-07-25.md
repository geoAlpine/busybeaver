## The last tool, identified exactly: `steps_rpad_dichotomy`

Working the tail bookkeeping through to the end pins the residue to ONE lemma.  The analysis:

### Why plain trimming is not enough

Write `(MEven h X).right = EBody h ++ (zeros (A h) ++ X)` — the free tail sits at the very end.
Then, with the cycles' own pad requirements,

    even at h:  IN right = EBody h ++ zeros (Pe h) ++ R     OUT right = OBody h ++ zeros (Bo h) ++ R
    odd  at h:  IN right = OBody h ++ zeros (Po h) ++ R     OUT right = EBody(h+1) ++ zeros (Be h) ++ R

and the arithmetic is decisive:

    Pe h = 73 + 2·padLen 5 (2h+5) + 2·2^(2h+10) + 2^(2h+11)
    Bo h = 41 +   padLen 5 (2h+5) +   2^(2h+10) + 2^(2h+11)
    Bo h − Pe h = −32 − padLen 5 (2h+5) − 2^(2h+10)  <  0

So an invariant of the form "right = `EBody h ++ zeros s`, `s ≥ Pe h`" **loses blanks every
generation** and cannot be maintained.  That is not a defect of the proof — it is the tape
representation: explicit blank cells are never created, the machine SYNTHESISES them past the end
of `right`.  The transports were proven WITH explicit pads, so they must be brought down to the
real (unpadded) config.

### Why `steps_right_mono` cannot do it

`steps_right_mono` gives `|R| + p ≤ |R'| + p'` — a LOWER bound on the OUT right length.  What the
trimming needs is that the `∃ i ≤ k` in `steps_runpad_zeros` does not exceed the OUT's own trailing
blank count, i.e. an UPPER bound.  Monotonicity is the wrong direction, exactly as it was for the
left in `hlow_j_ge`.

### The lemma that does do it

The right frontier is `frontR c = |c.right| + c.pos`.  Inspecting the two moves:

* `mvR` with `right = x :: rest` — frontier unchanged;  `mvL` — frontier unchanged;
* `mvR` with `right = []` — frontier **advances by 1**, and this is EXACTLY the step at which a
  trailing pad is absorbed.

So *pad absorbed* ⟺ *frontier advanced*, and the dichotomy is exact:

    steps_rpad_dichotomy :
      steps n ⟨s,p,⟨L,hd,R⟩⟩ = some ⟨s',p',⟨L',hd',R'⟩⟩ →
        ( steps n ⟨s,p,⟨L,hd,R ++ [false]⟩⟩ = some ⟨s',p',⟨L',hd',R' ++ [false]⟩⟩
            ∧ (R'.length : Int) + p' = (R.length : Int) + p )        -- pad rode, frontier still
      ∨   steps n ⟨s,p,⟨L,hd,R ++ [false]⟩⟩ = some ⟨s',p',⟨L',hd',R'⟩⟩  -- pad absorbed

Induction on `n`: once absorbed the two configs are EQUAL, so determinism carries the rest; while
riding, `crtail` is preserved and the frontier is untouched by both `mvR`-nonempty and `mvL`.

**Consequence.**  Whenever the run's right frontier advances by at least `k`, all `k` pads are
absorbed and the padded and trimmed runs land on the SAME config.  For the two cycles this holds
with room to spare: the padded OUT frontier is `|OBody h| + Bo h + q` while the trimmed IN frontier
is `|EBody h|`, and `|OBody h| ≫ |EBody h| + Pe h` because the doubling phase doubles the content.
So the trimmed cycle lands on the FULL `OBody h ++ zeros (Bo h)` — the invariant

    ∃ h s m p,  c = ⟨E, p, ⟨zeros m, false, EBody h ++ zeros s⟩⟩

is then preserved for **every** `s ≥ 0`, and `nonhalt_of_invariant` closes the chain.

The left twin (`steps_lpad_dichotomy`) is the same lemma mirrored, and it also retro-fits R5:
it would pin `j = 10` exactly, though R5 no longer needs pinning.
