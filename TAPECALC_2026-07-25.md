## The two exact congruences, stated once

> A trailing pad is ABSORBED exactly when the corresponding frontier ADVANCES.
> Right frontier `|right| + pos`; left frontier `|left| − pos`.  Both are monotone
> (`steps_{right,left}_mono`), and each advances only on `mvR` from an empty `right`
> (resp. `mvL` from an empty `left`) — which is precisely the pad-absorbing step.

Consequences, available to any machine:
* `steps_rpad_zeros_absorb` / `steps_lpad_zeros_absorb` — if the frontier advances by `≥ k`, a
  whole block of `k` pads is absorbed and the padded and trimmed runs land on the SAME config
  (no `∃ i` slack).  This is what carries a transport proven with explicit pads down to a real,
  unpadded reached configuration.
* the `q`-pinning corollary — a transport that is `∀` in its trailing tail pins its own
  translation, because the `+1`-pad instance forces the riding branch.  (Instantiated for `x2` as
  `cycleEven_pos` / `cycleOdd_pos`; the generic statement is one line from `steps_rpad_dich`.)
