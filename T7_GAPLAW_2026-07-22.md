## 3. What FAILED — the boundary is g-dependent `[MEASURED]`

The forward prediction of **M1(4)'s location was wrong**, and the reason is now localized:

- **`gap(11)` deviates by exactly +80.** Measured `4 188 247` against the law's `4 188 167`. This
  is the first gap that only exists at g=3 (at g=2, k=11 was the top rung, so there is no `gap(11)`
  to compare). Consequently the k=12 rung sits at 9 206 363, **+80** from prediction — the rung is
  real (`regenIn 12` confirmed, left block `1^4093 = 2^12−3`), just displaced.
- **The head is NOT a g-constant.** g=2: 6 580 steps from `M6(2)` to the k=5 rung. g=3: **53 382**.
- **The top rung's landing is not `cascadeReg k` at `exitSteps k`.** At g=3,
  `s₁₂ + exitSteps(12) = 11 329 117` is state **D**, right `= 1, 0⁴, 1^4093, 0², 1^2045, …` — the
  right blocks are there but the framing is shifted from `cascadeReg 12`'s `0³, 1^4093, 0², …`.
  At g=2 the top rung (k=11) *did* land cleanly. So top-rung behaviour differs between generations.

**Do not extrapolate the accounting of §2 to `∀g` — it is verified at g=2 only.** The interior law
of §1 is verified at two generations; the boundary (head, top rung, tail) is not.

## 4. What this is worth

- **T7's interior is solved-shaped.** The ladder is `gap(5), REGEN(5), gap(6), REGEN(6), …` with
  **two closed forms** — `exitSteps k = 2^{2k−3} + k·2^{k−1} + 2^{k−2} + 2` (already a Lean
  definition, `X2.lean:4316`) and `gap(k) = 4^k − 3·2^k + 7` (new, measured). That is precisely the
  k-independent-seam structure that let `§5bh`/`§5bj` close, and it is what a `∀k` Lean recursion
  would be built on.
- **The residual is now the BOUNDARY, not the bulk.** What remains unexplained is: the entry head,
  the `+80` at `gap(11)`, and the top-rung exit. These are three small, concrete, measurable
  objects — not a Θ(2^{2K}) fog.
- **Honest caveat that must travel with this:** `gap(k)` is a *fit to measurements*, not derived
  from the machine. It has 12 exact confirmations and one exact failure. The next step is to
  **derive** it (the way `x2lead_rec.py`/`x2trail_rec.py` derived `leadRec_closed`/
  `trailSteps_closed`), which will also explain the `+80` — a fitted law that fails at one point is
  a law with a missing term, and the derivation is what supplies it.
